01 — The Challenge

When inventory decisions depend on estimation, the full picture is hard to see.

Inventory ordering was largely driven by estimation and manual review. With a large number of items, it was difficult to have a clear view of what needed to be ordered, what was already overstocked, what had become stagnant, and which items were approaching expiry.

The challenge was not only stockouts.
It was the absence of one clear, decision-ready inventory picture.
Order quantities were estimated.Replenishment decisions relied heavily on manual review.
Excess and stagnant inventory were not clearly prioritized.Items requiring attention were difficult to identify consistently.
Expiry risk was difficult to identify proactively.Expiry information needed to be viewed alongside inventory and movement.
Different reports contained different pieces of the inventory picture.The information existed, but it was spread across the system.
Reviewing hundreds of items manually made prioritization difficult.A structured approach was needed to move from review to action.
